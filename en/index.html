    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    The short version.

    What is KAVEO GOLF?+

    Meet-ups in chat, results in tables, and a dedicated app for everything else — KAVEO brings your golfing life together in one place.

    What can I already use in the app?+

    Already today you can play rounds live with your fellow players, use the rules helper, check the current weather for your course, and find friends. Find all the details on the Features page, and see what's coming next on the Roadmap.

    Is KAVEO GOLF free?+

    The app is free to use.

    Which platforms is KAVEO coming to?+

    iOS, Android, and as a web app.

    When does KAVEO GOLF launch?+

    That's not fixed yet — likely at the start of the new golf season.
